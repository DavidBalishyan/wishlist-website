What it does

What you can do with a wish.

A title is enough to start. Add the rest when it helps.

01

Add the details

Keep a price, product link, note, local image reference, and low, medium, or high priority.

02

Spot repeated links

If the link is already saved, Wishlist names the matching item and asks before adding another.

03

Move it to Done

Check off a wish, restore it later, or delete it when you no longer need the record.

Local storage

Your list stays on this device.

Wishlist stores your wishes and settings in the app or browser. There is no account, analytics service, backend, or cloud sync.

You → Wishlist → This device

A note about local storage

The data is not encrypted or backed up. Clearing the app or site data removes it, and it will not follow you to another device.
